Create a complete Thai commercial artwork appropriate to the locked execution
mode. It may be a performance advertisement, product-led commercial, retail
promotion, lifestyle execution, or restrained key visual. Do not default every
campaign to a dense retail poster, and do not return a plain photograph with
text added afterward.

Build the canvas around three essential visual masses:
1. one large display-headline mass,
2. one dominant product or model hero,
3. one restrained brand-identification area.

Add a supporting-information group only when approved content materially
improves comprehension or is explicitly mandatory. It must remain subordinate
to the headline and hero. Never add a feature grid, compatibility rail, icon
row, repeated price panel, oversized CTA bar, or bottom utility strip merely to
fill space.

Use a full-frame designed background as an active graphic stage. Construct it
from a dominant brand-color gradient, a clear floor or spatial plane, a
controlled bright area behind the hero, low-contrast atmospheric decoration,
and subtle edge falloff. The background must support text placement, product
separation, depth, and commercial energy; it must not look like an unrelated
generated environment.

Reserve approximately 15–25% of the canvas for the headline, 40–60% for the
hero, 0–18% for optional grouped offer or benefit information, and 3–6% for
the logo. Protect at least 20% genuine low-detail breathing room and keep a safe
margin of approximately 5% around the canvas.

Use bold Thai display typography with compact line spacing, natural Thai line
breaks, strong size contrast, and two controlled emphasis colors. Treat the
headline as a major graphic shape. Promotional executions may use a clean
outline, dimensional extrusion, or firm offset shadow, but all headline lines
must share one coherent effect system.

Integrate the supplied product accurately. Give it believable scale, clean
retouched edges, contact shadow, matching perspective, light wrap, ambient
color spill, and rim light derived from the background. It must not appear as
a pasted PNG or float without physical support.

Use one coherent lighting world: a clear key light, controlled fill, background
separation light, consistent cast shadows, and selective highlight emphasis.
The hero must carry the highest edge definition and local contrast. Supporting
elements must be calmer, smaller, or softer.

Organize supporting information into no more than one compact module unless the
active density rules explicitly permit two. Use one consistent badge, pill, or
card language with matching corner radius, stroke, shadow direction, and
spacing. Do not place every sentence in a separate box. Do not repeat the same
claim, compatibility statement, price, benefit, or CTA in multiple locations.

Treat the packet as a truth boundary, not an instruction to display every
field. `verifiedFacts` authorize accurate choices but are off-art by default.
Only the exact headline, a supplied CTA, explicitly required utility
information, and official brand identification are mandatory. Feature and
supporting copy remain optional unless the packet explicitly says otherwise.
When optional copy competes with the hero or makes mobile reading slower, omit
it.

Keep the CTA compact and secondary. It must never become the largest bottom
bar or compete with the headline, price, or product.

Use a disciplined commercial palette: one dominant brand color, one supporting
tonal family, one functional accent, and controlled white or neutral areas.
Apply the accent only to the most important word, number, offer, product detail,
or CTA.

Finish the artwork with unified color grading, realistic contact shadows,
selective depth blur, subtle texture or grain, controlled glow, clean Thai
typesetting, and strong mobile-feed readability. The result must feel like a
professionally composited Thai agency advertisement, not a stock image, not a
landing-page layout, and not a generic AI poster.
