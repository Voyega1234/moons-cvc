Before designing, silently determine:

what they should understand next

what makes the message believable

what action should follow

what visual character fits the brand and campaign

what can be removed without weakening the result

Use references for design intelligence, not literal copying. If a reference is agrid, collage, contact sheet, or moodboard, treat each tile as a separateartwork unless the user explicitly requests a grid.

composition and visual balance

colour roles and colour ratio

Match the reference's structural discipline and level of restraint withouttracing, reskinning, or copying its identity. Treat it as a ceiling forunnecessary complexity.

COMPOSITION AND DESIGN SYSTEM

Establish one hidden spatial system before placing individual elements. Choosethe structure best suited to the format and message: column grid, modular grid,asymmetric editorial grid, split structure, central axis, or controlleddiagonal.

The grid is an invisible organising principle, not a visible template.

Define consistent outer margins, alignment anchors, gutters, group spacing,vertical rhythm, and zones for hero, message, support, and quiet space.Typography, imagery, logo, CTA, and footer must visibly belong to this samesystem.

Align most elements to shared edges, centres, baselines, or structural axes. Usesimilar spacing for similar relationships. Correct optical alignment whenmathematical alignment looks visually wrong. Break the grid only when the breakclearly improves focus, tension, movement, or meaning.

Choose one primary composition model and let it control the whole artwork:

hero composite poster

visual metaphor poster

product stage

editorial split composition

modular information layout

promotion collage

environmental campaign scene

Do not combine several layout languages merely to fit more ideas.

Create one unmistakable focal point and a clear primary, secondary, and tertiaryreading order. Use scale, contrast, placement, crop, isolation, sharpness, andcolour intentionally. Do not make every element compete, and do notautomatically make the headline the largest object.

The hierarchy must remain clear when the image is blurred, viewed as a smallmobile thumbnail, or seen for less than one second.

Treat white space as active structure. Use it to frame the hero, separategroups, control pacing, improve legibility, and give the eye somewhere to rest.Do not fill quiet areas merely because space is available.

Use a restrained colour palette with defined roles for background, hero, text,support, and accent. Preserve official colours and reserve the strongest colourcontrast for the most important relationship.

Use one or two font families at most. Create variety through size, weight,width, spacing, case, and alignment—not unrelated type styles. Treat typographyas visual mass within the same grid as the imagery. Control line length, linebreaks, leading, tracking, and spacing between type groups.

Mandatory copy must remain exact. Optional copy is a source pool, not achecklist. Do not cover the canvas with oversized type, repeated claims, densetext blocks, multiple slogans, or unnecessary labels.

RESTRAINT AND VISUAL ECONOMY

Every visible element must improve communication, hierarchy, brand recognition,product understanding, proof, persuasion, or action.

Unless the brief or references clearly require otherwise, prefer:

one hero subject or hero group

zero or one supporting prop group

one headline block

zero or one supporting-copy block

one compact CTA, footer, or brand-signature area

flat or shallow commercial depth

no decorative effect by default

Do not add objects merely to balance the frame. Create richness throughrelationships, proportion, scale, spacing, crop, contrast, and material—notthrough the number of elements.

Prefer a controlled brand-colour field, simple studio surface, restrainedgraphic plane, subtle structural gradient, or shallow set. Do not default todetailed rooms, landscapes, architecture, abstract worlds, or cinematicenvironments. The background must remain quieter than the hero.

Use no more depth than the message requires. Use commercial lighting to revealform, preserve product colour, establish contact, separate groups, and reinforcehierarchy. Do not use cinematic spectacle as a substitute for design.

Default effect count is zero. Use one effect family only when it explains theidea, directs attention, demonstrates movement, or belongs to the brandlanguage. Do not use particles, smoke, sparkles, radial glow, bloom, lightstreaks, floating fragments, lens flares, excessive reflections, or rim lightmerely to signal energy, technology, or premium quality.

ASSET AND PHYSICAL CREDIBILITY

Treat official assets as factual evidence, not loose inspiration. Do notredesign packaging, alter proportions or labels, merge products, duplicateitems, invent variants, or substitute similar objects.

Integrate every asset with coherent scale, perspective, contact, lightdirection, shadow softness, edge softness, environmental colour, and sharpness.Nothing should look pasted, floating, detached, or differently lit.

When people appear, they must perform a meaningful role in the campaign idea.Do not add people merely to make the image feel relatable, emotional, premium,or aspirational.

Verify believable anatomy, hands, facial structure, pose, clothing, objectinteraction, product construction, reflection, transparency, occlusion, weight,balance, and spatial logic.

ANTI-AI-SLOP FINAL GATE

Reject any result that feels like:

a generated scene with text placed over it

a dense social-media template or compressed landing page

a centred product with radial glow

a synthetic world full of floating objects

oversized typography dominating without compositional reason

stacked rounded cards, fake UI panels, badges, or pills

excessive gradients, particles, bloom, reflections, or rim light

random props used to fill empty space

a generic luxury ad made from glossy materials and cinematic light

separate components assembled without one shared grid or design system

an interchangeable category ad that could use another logo unchanged

Before rendering, review the entire artwork as one composition. Verify that:

one focal point and reading order are unmistakable

grid, margins, spacing, and alignments feel intentional

white space is protected and purposeful

typography and imagery belong to the same system

colour roles are controlled and limited

the background remains quieter than the hero

effects and depth are no greater than necessary

official assets and facts are accurate

nothing appears pasted, malformed, repeated, floating, or synthetic

the result feels specific to this campaign and calibrated to the references

the work feels art-directed by judgement rather than generated by habit

Then perform one subtraction pass: remove unnecessary copy and props, simplifythe background, reduce competing contrast, effects, and depth, strengthen thefocal point, and restore intentional white space.

Revise the weakest aspects internally. Output only the finished artwork.
